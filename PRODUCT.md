# Skeptical

Product context for design work. Confirmed by the owner (Armaan Seth) across earlier sessions; copied from the live site, not invented.

## What it is
Skeptical is a free, independent Lincoln-Douglas debate team. Eight debaters, two coaches who both still compete (Armaan Seth, head coach; Nathan Shannon, assistant coach). Live site: https://skeptical.pages.dev

## Who it is for
Middle-school and high-school debaters deciding whether to join, and debaters deciding whether to coach or give one lecture.

## Voice
A debater talking, not a brand. Short sentences. Titles end in a period. No em dashes.

## Hard rules (every version)
- Nothing fabricated. Results are breaks and speaker awards only.
- Students are first name and last initial. Coaches are full names. No roster.
- The prep drive is described, never shown, never linked.
- No Emerging Tech, no "TOC bid", no brief vendors named, no Tabroom link.
- Instagram is skeptical_team. Discord invite and caselist as on the live site.

## Live-site look (versions 1 and 2 only)
#050505 / #f0f0ec / #8f8f8a, rule #262624. Bodoni Moda and Instrument Sans. Dither only on the head.
Rejected and not to return: marquees, scramble text, pulsing status dot, custom cursor, head parallax, grain, pills, eyebrow labels and small grey text, three-column cards, glossy buttons, stepped scroll lock, contact form on home, giant cut-off wordmark, right-to-left drift-ins, speech-clock timer, full-height footers, yellow, Geist.

## Draft 2 (this folder)
Seven versions, one route each, in one Vite + React app. 1 and 2 rebuild the "Laocoön, Bronze and Time" prompt as Skeptical under the live-site rules. 3 to 7 are new designs with design freedom; the hard rules above still apply.
